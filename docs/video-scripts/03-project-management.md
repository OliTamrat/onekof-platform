# Video 3: Project Management
**Duration:** ~2 minutes
**Audience:** Project managers, team leads
**Tone:** Practical, demonstrative

---

## SCENE 1 — Projects Overview (0:00–0:15)
**[SHOW: Projects page — list or board view]**

**NARRATION:**
"Onekof's project management is inspired by tools like Jira — but designed for Ethiopian workflows. Let's create and manage a project."

---

## SCENE 2 — Create a Project (0:15–0:50)
**[SHOW: Click "+ Create" → "Create Project"]**

**NARRATION:**
"Click 'Create' and select 'Create Project'. The project wizard walks you through six steps."

**[ACTION: Step through the wizard]**

"Start with the basics — give your project a name, a short key like 'WDI' for Water Dam & Irrigation, and choose a project type."

**[ACTION: Fill in name, key]**

"Next, set the department, compliance requirements, and team. Assign an owner and add team members."

**[ACTION: Click through steps]**

"Set your timeline — start date, target date, and priority level. Finally, choose a workflow template and pick an icon and color to make it recognizable."

---

## SCENE 3 — Project Views (0:50–1:20)
**[SHOW: Project page with tabs]**

**NARRATION:**
"Every project has multiple views. The Summary tab shows progress at a glance — completion rates, team activity, and upcoming milestones."

**[ACTION: Click 'List' tab]**
"The List view shows all issues in a sortable table — filter by status, priority, or assignee."

**[ACTION: Click 'Board' tab]**
"The Board view is your Kanban board — drag and drop tasks between columns: To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done."

**[ACTION: Click 'Timeline' tab]**
"The Timeline view tracks project health over time — progress percentage, days remaining, and whether you're on track or at risk."

---

## SCENE 4 — Project Settings (1:20–1:40)
**[SHOW: Project settings panel]**

**NARRATION:**
"Each project has its own settings. Manage members and their roles, configure notification preferences, and adjust workflow stages. You can also set up automated rules — like auto-assigning new issues or sending alerts when deadlines approach."

---

## SCENE 5 — Wrap Up (1:40–2:00)
**[SHOW: Dashboard with multiple projects visible]**

**NARRATION:**
"You can manage multiple projects simultaneously. The Projects section in your sidebar shows starred and recent projects for quick access. In the next video, we'll dive into issues and task management — the daily building blocks of your project work."
