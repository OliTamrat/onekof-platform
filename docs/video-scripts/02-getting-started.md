# Video 2: Getting Started & Onboarding
**Duration:** ~2 minutes
**Audience:** New users signing up for the first time
**Tone:** Friendly, step-by-step guide

---

## SCENE 1 — Sign Up (0:00–0:20)
**[SHOW: Sign-in page]**

**NARRATION:**
"Getting started with Onekof takes less than two minutes. Go to onekof.com and click 'Get Started'. Enter your name, email, and create a password. You can also sign in with Google or Microsoft."

**[ACTION: Show sign-up form being filled]**

## SCENE 2 — Choose Your Organization Type (0:20–0:45)
**[SHOW: Onboarding step 1 — organization type selector]**

**NARRATION:**
"First, tell us about your organization. Choose from Government Ministry, NGO, Private Business, or Education. Each type comes with pre-configured features tailored to your needs."

**[ACTION: Click 'Government Ministry']**

"For example, a government ministry gets compliance tracking, procurement workflows, and public budget transparency — features that don't make sense for a startup."

## SCENE 3 — Organization Details (0:45–1:05)
**[SHOW: Onboarding step 2 — org name, department, team size]**

**NARRATION:**
"Enter your organization name and department. Choose your team size. Then set your workspace URL — this becomes your team's unique address on Onekof."

**[ACTION: Fill in "Ministry of Water & Irrigation", select team size]**

## SCENE 4 — Calendar & Language (1:05–1:25)
**[SHOW: Onboarding step 3 — calendar preference, language]**

**NARRATION:**
"Choose your calendar preference — Ethiopian, Gregorian, or both displayed side by side. Then select your preferred language. Onekof supports English, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, and Somali."

## SCENE 5 — Your Dashboard (1:25–1:50)
**[SHOW: Dashboard after onboarding completes]**

**NARRATION:**
"And you're in. Your dashboard shows an overview of your workspace — projects, recent tasks, team activity, and quick stats. The sidebar on the left gives you access to everything: Projects, Issues, Calendar, Teams, Budget, Goals, and more."

"Each section expands with sub-pages. Click any item to dive deeper."
