# Video 5: Ethiopian Calendar
**Duration:** ~90 seconds
**Audience:** All users — this is a key differentiator
**Tone:** Proud, highlighting uniqueness

---

## SCENE 1 — Why It Matters (0:00–0:15)
**[SHOW: Calendar page]**

**NARRATION:**
"Ethiopia uses a unique calendar — 13 months, different year numbering, and a different new year. No international project management tool supports this. Onekof does."

---

## SCENE 2 — Dual Calendar View (0:15–0:40)
**[SHOW: Calendar page showing Ethiopian and Gregorian dates]**

**NARRATION:**
"Open the Calendar from your sidebar. You'll see your tasks and deadlines displayed with Ethiopian dates. During onboarding, you chose your preference — Ethiopian only, Gregorian only, or both displayed together."

**[ACTION: Show date displaying in Ethiopian format]**

"Due dates, milestones, and events all respect the Ethiopian calendar. When you set a deadline for Megabit 22, your team sees Megabit 22 — not March 31."

---

## SCENE 3 — Creating Events (0:40–1:00)
**[SHOW: Click on a date → Quick Add Event modal appears]**

**NARRATION:**
"Click any date to create a new event or task. Set the title, assign it to a team member, choose a priority, and add a reminder. The event appears on your calendar instantly."

**[ACTION: Fill in event details, click create]**

"Click any existing event to view its full details, edit it, or mark it complete."

---

## SCENE 4 — Fiscal Year (1:00–1:20)
**[SHOW: Budget page showing fiscal year]**

**NARRATION:**
"Onekof also respects the Ethiopian fiscal year — starting in July, not January. Budget tracking, financial reports, and quarterly goals all align with how Ethiopian organizations actually operate. No workarounds needed."

---

## SCENE 5 — Close (1:20–1:30)
**[SHOW: Calendar with events, Ethiopian dates visible]**

**NARRATION:**
"The Ethiopian calendar isn't a translation layer — it's built into the core of Onekof. Your dates, your calendar, your way."
