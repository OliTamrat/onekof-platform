# Video 4: Issues & Task Board
**Duration:** ~2 minutes
**Audience:** All team members
**Tone:** Hands-on, practical

---

## SCENE 1 — What Are Issues? (0:00–0:15)
**[SHOW: Issues board page with Kanban columns]**

**NARRATION:**
"Issues are the building blocks of your work in Onekof. They can be tasks, bugs, stories, or epics — anything your team needs to track and complete."

---

## SCENE 2 — Create an Issue (0:15–0:45)
**[SHOW: Click "+ Create Issue" button]**

**NARRATION:**
"Click 'Create Issue' to add a new task. Choose the project it belongs to, give it a title, and set the essentials — type, priority, and status."

**[ACTION: Fill in the form — title, select project, set priority to High]**

"Need more detail? Expand 'More fields' to add a description, assign it to a team member, set a due date, link it to goals, or add watchers who'll be notified of updates."

**[ACTION: Click "More fields", show expanded form]**

"Hit 'Create Issue' and it appears immediately on your board."

---

## SCENE 3 — The Kanban Board (0:45–1:15)
**[SHOW: Board view with tasks in columns]**

**NARRATION:**
"The board view organizes issues into columns by status — To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done. Drag a card from one column to another to update its status instantly."

**[ACTION: Drag a card from 'To Do' to 'In Progress']**

"Each card shows the key, title, assignee avatar, priority indicator, and due date — everything you need at a glance without opening it."

---

## SCENE 4 — Issue Detail Panel (1:15–1:45)
**[SHOW: Click on an issue card → slideout panel opens]**

**NARRATION:**
"Click any issue to open the detail panel. Here you can edit the description, change status and priority, update the due date, and manage assignees."

**[ACTION: Scroll through the detail panel]**

"Below that, you'll find subtasks — break large tasks into smaller steps. Add comments to discuss progress with your team. And the activity timeline shows every change made to the issue."

**[ACTION: Show subtasks, comments section]**

"Watchers automatically receive notifications when the issue is updated, assigned, or commented on."

---

## SCENE 5 — List View & Filters (1:45–2:00)
**[SHOW: Switch to List view]**

**NARRATION:**
"Switch to List view for a spreadsheet-like overview. Use filters to narrow down by status, priority, assignee, or project. Group by status or priority to organize your view. These are the same issues — just different ways to see them."

**[ACTION: Click filter buttons, show grouping]**
