# Video 7: Teams & Collaboration
**Duration:** ~90 seconds
**Audience:** Team leads, managers
**Tone:** Collaborative, organizational

---

## SCENE 1 — Teams Overview (0:00–0:15)
**[SHOW: Teams page — list of teams]**

**NARRATION:**
"Onekof helps you organize people by function, department, or project. Create teams, assign members, and track collaboration across your organization."

---

## SCENE 2 — Create a Team (0:15–0:35)
**[SHOW: Click "Create Team" button]**

**NARRATION:**
"Click 'Create Team' and give it a name — Engineering, Finance, Field Operations, whatever fits your structure. Add a description and choose an icon."

**[ACTION: Fill in team name, add members]**

"Then add members. Each person gets a role — Owner, Admin, Member, or Guest — controlling what they can see and do within the team."

---

## SCENE 3 — Team Activity (0:35–0:55)
**[SHOW: Teams activity page]**

**NARRATION:**
"The Activity tab shows what your team is working on — task completions, new assignments, comments, and member changes. It's a live pulse of your team's work."

**[ACTION: Scroll through activity feed]**

"Filter by team to focus on a specific group, or view across all teams for a company-wide picture."

---

## SCENE 4 — Members Management (0:55–1:15)
**[SHOW: Members page]**

**NARRATION:**
"The Members page shows everyone in your organization. Invite new members by email — they'll receive an invitation link to join your workspace."

**[ACTION: Show invite flow]**

"Manage roles, remove members, or reassign people across teams as your organization evolves."

---

## SCENE 5 — Close (1:15–1:30)
**[SHOW: Team overview with stats]**

**NARRATION:**
"Strong project management starts with organized teams. Set up your team structure in Onekof and give everyone clarity on who's doing what."
