# Onekof Video Scripts — Production Guide

## For use with Synthesia or screen recording

### Video Series Structure
Each script includes: scene descriptions, narration text, and on-screen action cues.
Scripts are written for a professional presenter tone — warm but authoritative.

### Videos in this series:

| # | Title | Duration | Audience |
|---|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Platform Introduction | ~90 sec | New users, investors |
| 2 | Getting Started & Onboarding | ~2 min | New users |
| 3 | Project Management | ~2 min | Project managers |
| 4 | Issues & Task Board | ~2 min | Team members |
| 5 | Ethiopian Calendar | ~90 sec | All users |
| 6 | Multi-Language Support | ~60 sec | All users |
| 7 | Teams & Collaboration | ~90 sec | Team leads |
| 8 | Budget & Finance | ~90 sec | Finance teams |
| 9 | Knowledge Base & Wiki | ~90 sec | All users |
| 10 | Reports & Analytics | ~90 sec | Managers |

### Synthesia Tips
- Upload each script as a separate video
- Use "Professional" avatar style
- Set background to Onekof dark theme screenshots
- Add Onekof logo watermark in corner
- For Amharic version: translate scripts and use Synthesia's multilingual voice
