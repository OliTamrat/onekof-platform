Why Onekof

Built for how you actually work

Not another Silicon Valley clone. Onekof is designed from the ground up for Ethiopian and East African teams, calendars, currencies, and languages.

📅

Ethiopian Calendar

Native Ethiopian calendar support with automatic Gregorian conversion. Plan sprints, set deadlines, and track milestones in the calendar your team knows.

🌐

5 Languages

Full interface in English, Amharic, Afan Oromo, Tigrinya, and Somali. Switch instantly. Every button, label, and notification — translated.

💰

ETB Budgets

Track project budgets in Ethiopian Birr with department allocation, expense tracking, and fiscal year alignment starting in July.

🤖

AI Documents

Generate meeting notes, project briefs, and reports with AI assistance. Understands your project context and team structure.

Automations

Automate repetitive workflows: auto-assign issues, trigger notifications, update statuses. Build rules without writing code.

🔒

Data Sovereignty

Your data stays where you need it. Three-tier hosting: global cloud, private on-premise in Ethiopia, or government-grade EthioTelecom Cloud.
