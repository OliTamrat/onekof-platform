# ADR-0004 — The Medical module: build properly, do not withdraw

**Status:** accepted (design PROPOSED, M1–M8 pending) · **Date:** 2026-07-28

## Context
Founder testing exposed that Healthcare orgs were promised "facility
management, medical projects, compliance tracking" while the medical and
compliance sections were dead switches over ~19-line placeholders. Two
honest exits: strip the promise, or build the vertical.

## Decision
Founder decision: build it properly, with design-doc discipline —
`architecture/MEDICAL_MODULE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (v1.0 PROPOSED, decisions
M1–M8 awaiting approval), `architecture/M1_ACTIVATION_RUNBOOK.md` for the
first activation.

## Consequences
Until M1–M8 are approved this ADR is the record that the gap is known
and chosen-into, not overlooked. Supersede on approval.
