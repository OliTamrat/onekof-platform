### Description (max 4000 chars)
```
Onekof is the modern project management platform built for high-performing teams — from government ministries to fast-moving startups.

Whether you're tracking a national infrastructure project or shipping a product sprint, Onekof gives your team one place to plan, execute, and deliver.

── MANAGE WORK, YOUR WAY ──

Create issues, assign owners, set priorities, and track progress across Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done — all in a clean, intuitive interface built for speed.

Visualize your project on a Kanban board or dive into list view. Filter by priority, assignee, type, or due date. Search everything in seconds.

── REAL-TIME TEAM COLLABORATION ──

Stay in sync with your entire team. Watch issues, get notified the moment something changes, and respond from wherever you are. Mention teammates, leave comments, and attach files — all from your phone.

── BUDGET & FINANCIAL TRACKING ──

Track budgets at the project and organization level. Log expenses, monitor burn rate, and drill into spend by category. Onekof's AI receipt scanner reads your invoices and automatically extracts line items, vendor details, and totals — saving your team hours of manual entry.

── DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE ──

Upload contracts, reports, and invoices. Our AI engine processes your documents, generates summaries, and extracts structured budget data with a confidence score — giving your team instant insight without reading every page.

── GOALS & MILESTONES ──

Set organizational goals, link them to projects, and track progress with visual indicators. Keep leadership aligned on what matters most.

── BUILT FOR AFRICA ──

Onekof supports five languages natively: English, Amharic (አማርኛ), Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya (ትግርኛ), and Soomaali. Switch languages instantly from settings.

Designed with Ethiopian teams in mind, Onekof respects data sovereignty with a three-tier hosting architecture — your government data stays where it belongs.

── SECURITY FIRST ──

Protect your account with Face ID or fingerprint biometric lock. Your session is secured with JWT authentication and your credentials are stored in the device's secure enclave — never in plain text.

── WORKS OFFLINE ──

Lose connection? Onekof keeps you in the app. Your session persists offline, and queued actions sync automatically when you reconnect.

── EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE ──

- Dashboard with live stats and activity feed
- Full issue lifecycle management
- Kanban boards per project
- Team & member management
- Budget tracking with AI receipt scanning
- Document management with AI summaries
- Goal & milestone tracking
- Organization-wide calendar
- Notification center with smart filters
- Biometric app lock
- Push notifications
- 5-language support

Onekof works seamlessly with your organization's web platform at onekof.com. Create an account on the web and sign in to the app — your data is waiting.

────────────────────────────
Questions? support@onekof.com
Privacy: onekof.com/privacy
Terms: onekof.com/terms
────────────────────────────
```
