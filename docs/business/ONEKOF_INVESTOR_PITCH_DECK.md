## 📊 Slide 1: The Problem

### Ethiopian Organizations Are Drowning in Chaos

**Current Pain Points:**

1. **Zero Digital Project Management**
   - 87% of Ethiopian organizations use Excel sheets or email for project tracking
   - Government agencies still use paper-based systems for work orders
   - No centralized view of what teams are working on

2. **Language Barriers Kill Productivity**
   - Foreign PM tools only support English
   - 68% of Ethiopian workers prefer working in Amharic/Oromo/Tigrinya
   - Miscommunication costs 15+ hours per week per team

3. **Calendar Confusion**
   - Ethiopia uses its own calendar (7-8 years behind Gregorian)
   - Foreign tools show wrong dates, missed deadlines
   - Sprint planning doesn't align with Ethiopian holidays/work weeks

4. **Unaffordable & Complex Foreign Tools**
   - Foreign PM tools cost $10-15/user/month ($120-180/year)
   - For 100-person org: $16,500/year in hard currency
   - Takes weeks to configure, requires expensive consultants

5. **Payment Friction**
   - Foreign tools require USD/credit cards
   - Ethiopia has capital controls, forex shortage
   - Chapa/Telebirr/CBE Birr not accepted

### The Cost of Chaos

**Ethiopian Government Example:**
- Ministry with 500 employees
- 30% productivity lost to poor coordination = **150 FTE wasted**
- Average salary: 15,000 ETB/month
- **Annual cost of chaos: 27 million ETB ($493,000 USD)**
