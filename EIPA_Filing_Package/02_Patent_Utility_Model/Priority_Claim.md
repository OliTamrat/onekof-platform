# Priority Claim

## Is a Priority Claim Applicable?
- [ ] **Yes** - I have a prior filing in another country
- [ ] **No** - This is my first filing anywhere (skip this document)

---

## Priority Claim Details

A priority claim allows you to claim the filing date of an earlier application
filed in another country, provided you file in Ethiopia within 12 months of the
original filing date (Paris Convention).

- [ ] Translation into Amharic or English (if in another language)

## Notes
- If you have NOT filed in any other country, this document is not required
- The priority date becomes your effective filing date in Ethiopia
- Consult with an IP attorney if you have filings in multiple countries
