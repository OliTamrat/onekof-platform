# Goods and Services - Nice Classification

## What is Nice Classification?
The Nice Classification is an international system for categorizing goods and services
for trademark registration purposes. You must identify the specific classes that
cover your products/services.

---

## Recommended Classes for OneKof Platform

### Class 9 - Computer Software
**Goods:**
- Downloadable computer software for project management
- Downloadable computer software for team collaboration
- Downloadable mobile applications for organizational management
- Computer software platforms, recorded or downloadable

### Class 35 - Business Management & Administration
**Services:**
- Business project management services
- Providing an online platform for business management and collaboration
- Business data analysis services
- Administrative management of organizational workflows
- Providing business information via a website

### Class 42 - Scientific & Technological Services
**Services:**
- Software as a Service (SaaS) featuring software for project management
- Software as a Service (SaaS) featuring software for team collaboration
- Cloud computing services
- Platform as a Service (PaaS) featuring computer software platforms
  for organizational management
- Computer software development
- Hosting of digital content on the Internet
- Design and development of computer software

---

## Filing Notes
- You must pay a separate fee for EACH class
- Be specific in listing goods/services (avoid overly broad descriptions)
- Only claim classes where you currently use OR intend to use the mark
- Consult the full Nice Classification list at WIPO for additional classes if needed
